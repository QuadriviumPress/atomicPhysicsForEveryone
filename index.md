*Atomic Physics for Everyone: An Introduction to Atomic Physics, Quantum
Mechanics, and Precision Spectroscopy with No College-Level Prerequisites*
is an open-access book by **Will Raven** (Department of Physics, Smith
College), published by Springer in 2025. Assuming no college-level
prerequisites, it builds intuition for atoms and light, natural light,
atoms at rest and in motion, saturated absorption spectroscopy, quantum
mechanics, angular momentum, electronic structure, hyperfine structure,
isotope shifts and nuclear forces, and the Standard Model of particle
physics — with learning goals, worked examples, and end-of-chapter
problems.

This is a faithful MyST Markdown conversion of the printed book: all
equations are live LaTeX, all figures are the original book artwork, and
chapter references and appendices are included.
